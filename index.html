K9 One Second Science

K9 One Second Science is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel One Second Science.

It runs on the owner's own computer and uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • confirm which YouTube channel the owner has signed in to;
  • upload short science videos that the owner has reviewed and approved, with their title, description, thumbnail and scheduled publish time;
  • read public statistics (views, likes, comments) of the channel's own videos.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.
