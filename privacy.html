Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy covers K9 One Second Science ("the app"), a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel One Second Science.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube channel identity (youtube.readonly): the channel ID and title, to make sure videos go to the correct channel.
  • Video upload (youtube.upload): to upload videos the channel owner has approved, and to set their thumbnail and schedule.
  • Public video statistics: view, like and comment counts of the channel's own videos.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a folder restricted to that user. The app has no server, database or analytics service, and does not send Google user data anywhere except to Google's own APIs.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to any third party, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.

Contact

Through the One Second Science channel page.
